# Commitment Intelligence

## Purpose

Define the commitment intelligence foundation of ST AI OS.

This module establishes how ST AI OS identifies, evaluates, and develops commitment throughout the customer journey.

## Philosophy

ST AI OS recognizes that commitment is developed progressively through trust, readiness, participation, and consistent engagement rather than pressure or persuasion.

## Core Principles

Commitment Intelligence continuously seeks to:

- Identify commitment signals.
- Measure readiness for action.
- Recognize planning participation.
- Detect commitment opportunities.
- Recommend appropriate next actions.

## Scope

This module governs commitment evaluation across all AI Agents.

Specialized commitment models and scoring systems are implemented in separate modules within this layer.

## Stability

This document defines the stable commitment intelligence foundation of ST AI OS and should evolve only through validated field experience and operational learning.
