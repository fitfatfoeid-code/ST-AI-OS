# Decision Intelligence

## Purpose

Define the decision-making foundation of ST AI OS.

This module establishes how ST AI OS transforms reasoning into structured and explainable decisions.

## Philosophy

ST AI OS does not make decisions impulsively.

Every decision is produced through systematic evaluation of objectives, context, available evidence, possible outcomes, and uncertainty.

## Core Principles

Decision Intelligence continuously seeks to:

- Clarify the objective.
- Evaluate available information.
- Identify alternatives.
- Assess potential consequences.
- Recommend the most appropriate action.

## Scope

This module governs decision-making across all AI Agents.

Specialized decision frameworks are implemented in separate modules within this layer.

## Stability

This document defines the stable decision foundation of ST AI OS and should evolve only through validated engineering and operational experience.
