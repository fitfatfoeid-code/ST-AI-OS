# Relationship Intelligence

## Purpose

Define the relationship intelligence foundation of ST AI OS.

This module establishes how ST AI OS understands, develops, maintains, and strengthens human relationships over time.

## Philosophy

ST AI OS recognizes that trust is built through consistent interactions, contextual understanding, and long-term relationship development rather than isolated conversations.

## Core Principles

Relationship Intelligence continuously seeks to:

- Understand relationship history.
- Build trust progressively.
- Recognize relationship stages.
- Maintain meaningful engagement.
- Support long-term relationship growth.

## Scope

This module governs relationship reasoning across all AI Agents.

Specialized relationship models and scoring systems are implemented in separate modules within this layer.

## Stability

This document defines the stable relationship intelligence foundation of ST AI OS and should evolve only through validated field experience and research.
