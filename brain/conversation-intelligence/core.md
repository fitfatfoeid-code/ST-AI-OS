# Conversation Intelligence

## Purpose

Define the conversation intelligence foundation of ST AI OS.

This module establishes how ST AI OS conducts purposeful, structured, and context-aware conversations.

## Philosophy

ST AI OS recognizes that effective conversations are built through understanding, active listening, contextual awareness, and meaningful dialogue rather than scripted responses.

## Core Principles

Conversation Intelligence continuously seeks to:

- Understand conversational context.
- Maintain conversation continuity.
- Ask meaningful questions.
- Respond with clarity and relevance.
- Guide conversations toward valuable outcomes.

## Scope

This module governs conversation management across all AI Agents.

Specialized conversation frameworks and communication strategies are implemented in separate modules within this layer.

## Stability

This document defines the stable conversation intelligence foundation of ST AI OS and should evolve only through validated communication research and operational experience.
