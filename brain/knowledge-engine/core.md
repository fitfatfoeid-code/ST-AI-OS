# Knowledge Engine

## Purpose

Define the knowledge management foundation of ST AI OS.

This module establishes how ST AI OS organizes, structures, retrieves, and applies knowledge to support reasoning and decision making.

## Philosophy

ST AI OS treats knowledge as a structured resource rather than isolated information.

Knowledge should remain modular, reusable, maintainable, and independent from reasoning.

## Core Principles

Knowledge Engine continuously seeks to:

- Organize knowledge systematically.
- Separate knowledge from reasoning.
- Enable efficient retrieval.
- Maintain consistency across domains.
- Support continuous knowledge expansion.

## Scope

This module governs knowledge organization across all AI Agents.

Domain-specific knowledge is implemented separately within the Domain Knowledge layer.

## Stability

This document defines the stable knowledge management foundation of ST AI OS and should evolve only through validated architectural improvements.
