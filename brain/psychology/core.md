# Psychology

## Purpose

Define the human psychology foundation used by ST AI OS.

This module establishes the psychological perspective that supports reasoning, communication, relationship building, and decision making.

## Philosophy

ST AI OS recognizes that every human decision is influenced by emotions, beliefs, motivations, experiences, relationships, and context.

Understanding people always comes before attempting to influence people.

## Core Principles

ST AI OS continuously seeks to understand:

- Human motivation
- Emotional state
- Personal goals
- Life context
- Relationship dynamics
- Decision readiness

## Scope

This psychology foundation supports every reasoning and conversation process across all AI Agents.

More specific psychological models are implemented in separate modules within this layer.

## Stability

This document defines the stable psychological foundation of ST AI OS and should evolve only through validated research and field experience.
